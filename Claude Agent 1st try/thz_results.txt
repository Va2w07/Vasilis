  (measured magnitudes sit below these because the echo is also attenuated

CAVEATS
  * The reference scan carries its own etalon at +1.5 and +3.6 ps (~3 % of
    peak) from optics in the beam path. It sits inside the main-pulse gate
    and is the dominant systematic on the extracted absorption.
  * |echo/main| is non-monotonic in frequency (rises between 0.6 and 1.0 THz),
    which is unphysical for pure absorption. Until that is understood, treat
    alpha as indicative; n is unaffected, being phase-derived.
  * The record is truncated while ~1 % ringing is still present, setting a
    floor on fringe-fitting accuracy and hence on the thicknesses.
  * Si absorption is near the limit this dynamic range can resolve; small
    alpha_Si is an upper bound, not a measurement.
